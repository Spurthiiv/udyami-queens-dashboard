    How this page works
    1. Record the order. When a customer orders from you (on the app, WhatsApp or phone), choose each product and quantity, then press Add item.
    2. Add the delivery area and payment type, then press Create order. Stock goes down straight away.
    3. Accept the order when you start working on it. It moves to Packing.
    4. Hand to rider once it's packed and picked up.
    5. Mark delivered when the customer has it. Only delivered orders count toward sales and payouts.
    6. If an order can't go ahead, press Cancel twice. The stock goes back on your shelf.

    How this page works
    1. Add each product you sell with its name, category, selling price and how many you have ready.
    2. Set the reorder level. When stock drops to this number, the product shows up in Stock alerts.
    3. Change a price by typing a new amount on the product card. It saves when you leave the box.
    4. Use the switch to hide a product you can't make for a while without deleting it.
    5. Delete removes the product for good (press it twice). Past orders keep their details.

    How this page works
    1. Check the status column. Products at zero are out of stock; products at or below their reorder level are running low. Both are listed first.
    2. Use − and + to correct a count by one, for example after a spoiled jar.
    3. Type a number in "Add stock" and press Add when you've made a fresh batch.
    4. Change "Reorder at" to decide when a product should warn you.
    5. Stock also changes on its own: it goes down when you create an order and back up if you cancel one.

    How this page works
    1. Set your platform fee and monthly goal in Shop settings. They're used for every number here.
    2. Only delivered orders count. Each week runs Monday to Sunday.
    3. Your payout is the week's sales minus the platform fee.

    5. Copy as CSV copies the table so you can paste it into a spreadsheet for your accountant.

    1. Record feedback whenever a customer rates you or sends a message about a product.
